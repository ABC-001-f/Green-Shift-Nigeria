    Stay safe while you shift

    Carbon monoxideNever run a generator indoors, in a garage or on a balcony. If someone feels dizzy near one, get them into fresh air and seek help.
    Fuel firesSwitch off and let the engine cool before refuelling. Never store petrol in living areas.
    BackfeedingNever plug a generator into a wall socket. Have an electrician fit a changeover switch.
    Batteries & e-wasteKeep batteries ventilated. Never burn or dump them; return them to a licensed recycler.

    Project story

    Built to be used, not just admired.

    Problem

    Nigerian homes and shops lose money and health to petrol generators, and plastic-blocked drains turn ordinary rain into floods. People know it is bad but have no numbers to act on.

    Who it’s for

    Households and small businesses on generators, residents who live near drains, and LGA sanitation teams that need to decide where to send crews first.

    Approach

    Start from a real behaviour, measure it with transparent formulas, show the next step, and keep score so progress is visible.

    Environmental benefit

    One 3 kVA home moving to solar avoids about 6.7 t CO₂ a year. Clearing drains before storms keeps plastic out of waterways and prevents flood losses.

    Tools

    HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, Canvas for the city scene, and SVG for the charts and radar. No backend and no tracking; nothing leaves the browser.

    Limitations

    Drain data and the photo scan are simulated. Fuel, sunshine and solar costs are planning estimates, not quotes. Health meters are illustrative.

    Assumptions you can change
    • Sunshine hours are broad regional estimates, not site measurements.
    • Petrol prices are held flat for five years. If they rise, solar pays back sooner.
    • Maintenance, repairs, battery replacement and grid supply are not included.
    • CO₂ uses a standard tailpipe factor and ignores solar equipment life-cycle emissions.
